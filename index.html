Introduction

Welcome to DoBu Martial Arts

A professional martial arts and fitness gym where discipline, confidence and personal development come together.

Train, Learn and Grow

DoBu Martial Arts provides a welcoming training environment for beginners, experienced practitioners and junior members. Our large matted martial arts area supports a range of traditional and modern training sessions.

Alongside martial arts classes, members can access a fully equipped fitness room, sauna, steam room and changing and shower facilities. Specialist self-defence and fitness training are also available.

Whether your goal is to improve fitness, develop martial arts skills, build confidence or learn practical self-defence, DoBu offers a structured environment supported by qualified instructors.
