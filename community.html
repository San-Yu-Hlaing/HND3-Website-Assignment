Welcome

More Than Training Partners

The DoBu community is a supportive network of martial artists at every level. Whether you're sharing a training tip, celebrating a grading, or organising a social event — this is your space.

Members with an active account can post discussions, like posts, and connect through our integrated social channels.

Grading events bring the community together. Members can celebrate new belt achievements, support training partners, and share progress with coaches and friends. These events are a key part of the DoBu community experience beyond regular classes.

Training Tips

Warm Up Properly

Spend 10 minutes on dynamic stretching before every class to reduce injury risk and improve performance.

Drill, Don't Just Roll

Dedicate at least one open mat session per week to drilling specific techniques rather than only sparring.

Recovery Matters

Use the sauna and steam room after intense sessions. Elite members have full access — take advantage of it.
