About DoBu Martial Arts

Discover our story, our values, and the team behind Manchester's premier martial arts gym.

Our Story

Built on Discipline & Respect

Founded in 2010 by head coach Mauricio Gomez, DoBu Martial Arts has grown from a small community dojo into one of Manchester's most respected martial arts gyms. Our name — DoBu — reflects our philosophy: the way (Do) of martial excellence through persistent effort (Bu).

We believe martial arts training transforms lives. Whether you're a complete beginner looking to build confidence, a parent seeking a positive activity for your child, or an experienced practitioner preparing for competition, DoBu provides the environment, coaching, and community to help you succeed.

Purpose

Our Mission

🎯

Empower Through Training

To provide accessible, high-quality martial arts and fitness training that empowers individuals of all ages and abilities to develop physical skills, mental resilience, and personal confidence.

🤝

Build Community

To foster a supportive, inclusive community where every member feels valued — from first-day beginners to seasoned black belts competing at national level.

Principles

Our Values

The core principles that guide everything we do at DoBu Martial Arts.

  • Respect — For coaches, training partners, the dojo, and ourselves in every session.
  • Discipline — Consistent effort and commitment are the foundation of all progress.
  • Excellence — We strive for the highest standards in coaching, facilities, and member experience.
  • Inclusivity — Martial arts is for everyone regardless of age, gender, background, or ability level.
  • Safety — Structured, supervised training with qualified coaches in a properly equipped environment.
  • Community — We grow stronger together through shared goals, events, and mutual support.
Facilities

Training Environment

A purpose-built facility designed for martial arts excellence and member comfort.

Martial Arts Dojo

Our spacious matted area accommodates multiple classes simultaneously, with professional-grade tatami suitable for throws, grappling, and striking disciplines.

Fitness Gym

Free weights, resistance machines, cardio equipment, and functional training zones — all maintained to the highest standards for combat athlete conditioning.

Recovery & Amenities

Sauna, steam room, spacious changing rooms, showers, and secure lockers — everything you need before and after training.
