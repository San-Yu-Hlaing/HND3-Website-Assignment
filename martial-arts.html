Our Martial Arts

Four world-class disciplines plus fitness and self-defence — taught by accredited black belt instructors.

Ground Fighting

Brazilian Jiu-jitsu

Brazilian Jiu-jitsu (BJJ) focuses on ground fighting, submissions, and positional control. Using leverage and technique rather than strength, BJJ enables smaller practitioners to defend against larger opponents effectively.

Our programme covers guard work, sweeps, submissions, takedowns, and competition preparation under the guidance of 3rd Dan coach Mauricio Gomez and 2nd Dan assistant Guy Victory.

Training focus: Ground positions, submissions, live rolling, competition strategy.

Grappling

Judo

Judo, meaning "the gentle way," teaches throws, takedowns, pins, and submission holds. Developed in Japan, judo emphasises using an opponent's momentum against them — making it one of the most effective martial arts for self-defence.

Head coach Mauricio Gomez holds a 4th Dan Black Belt and brings decades of competition and coaching experience to every session.

Balance & Coordination Self-Defence Olympic Sport Discipline
Beginners Welcome Intermediate Advanced / Competition

Training focus: Throwing techniques (nage-waza), groundwork (ne-waza), randori sparring.

Striking

Karate

Karate develops powerful striking techniques using punches, kicks, knee strikes, and elbow strikes. Our programme combines traditional kata forms with practical kumite (sparring) to build both technical precision and real-world application.

5th Dan coach Sarah Nova leads our karate programme, with Morris Davis (3rd Dan) supporting advanced and competition classes.

Striking

Muay Thai

Known as the "Art of Eight Limbs," Muay Thai uses fists, elbows, knees, and shins in devastating combination. Our accredited coaches deliver authentic Thai boxing training suitable for fitness, self-defence, and competition.

Accredited Muay Thai coaches Mauricio Gomez and Morris Davis bring competition experience and technical expertise to every class.
